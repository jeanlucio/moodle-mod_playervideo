**PlayerVideo** turns a YouTube, Vimeo or self-hosted video into an interactive Moodle activity:
teachers mark questions, notes and polls directly on the video's own timeline — pulling
questions from the real Question Bank or generating them with AI from a pasted transcript — and
students answer inline as the video plays, with auto-save, resume, anti-skip and a full grading
and analytics workflow.
